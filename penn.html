Leaf Sensor Detection

This was for my research at University of Pennsylvania IoT4Ag REU funded by the National Science Foundation. The center focuses on using technology for agricultural efficiency.

This was my research poster.

I developed an automated hyperspectral imaging pipeline in Python using the Ximea API and deployed on-device machine learning on the NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX for The Beast, a self-driving agricultural robot.

I proposed and led a project to correct hyperspectral angle-detection errors, building a calibration model that extended sensor measurement capability from 0° to ±40° with only 1.3° error, enabling more reliable analysis of optical metasurface-based colorimetric leaf sensors. My work also contributed to characterizing sensor uniformity, evaluating alternative low-cost sensor substrates, and improving the robustness of the full hyperspectral acquisition system.

In terms of the hyperspectral camera, I couldn't fully get it working but they found a PhD candidate to inherit this project.
