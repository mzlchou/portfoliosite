About Me Personally

Hey my name is Michael and I thought I could introduce myself a little bit more here. I love music, I've been playing violin since I was 2 years old, and I realize that it really has affected all the music related projects that I create. I used to perform in Brazil, Canada and across the US and I still play (not as much as before) in the Tufts University orchestra.

In my free time, I love being active. I love playing tennis, squash, hiking, and skiing. My favorite place to ski is in Steamboat Colorado. To relax, my friends and I love cooking, watching sports (mainly football and tennis), playing video games, and rewatching the Star Wars movies together.

My goal in my career is to create technology that has some sort of positive impact on people. Right now, I really enjoy the feeling of teaching people things I'm good at so expanding that "positive impact feeling" to thousands to millions of people is what I'm after in my career.
