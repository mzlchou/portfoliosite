Malawi Water Tank Level Tracker

This project is also for Tufts Engineers without Borders. The previous project was for the local greenhouse we built, but our club focuses on international projects as well (we do work for communities in Malawi and Ecuador). The project lead asked me to head a project for measuring water level in a tank and sending the data to the United States for remote monitoring via SMS.

Currently I am ordering parts. I decided on using blues because they have STM32 MCU's pre-integrated with a SIM card module and international connectivity.
