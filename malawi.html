Malawi Water Tank Level Tracker

This project is also for Tufts Engineers without Borders. This is for the community we partner with in Malawi. I led a project for measuring water level in a tank and sending the data to the United States for remote monitoring via cellular.

The device is solar powered, which charges a battery which we use to power our microcontroller (ESP32C3) and our cellular unit from Blues. We chose Blues as they guaranteed that the cellular signal works in both United States (for testing) and Malawi for implementation in addition to the interface being easy to use. The ESP32 stores 24 ultrasonic sensor readings (once an hour) to determine how full the tank is, and then stores it in an SD card file. At the end of the day, the microcontroller sends the file via serial to the Blues cellular communication unit to send to the Blues Notehub. Notehub is routed to HTTP endpoint on our website for viewing.
