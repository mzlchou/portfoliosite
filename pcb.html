Wireless Greenhouse Monitoring System

In Engineers without Borders (EWB), we built a fully automated and self power sufficient on campus greenhouse. I helped build the automated watering system my freshman year. The following semester I became a technical lead and lead a group of 10+ students to create a wireless monitoring system. I am now the Vice President of Tufts EWB.

This is a picture of the old design (will update with a better photo)

The original design was created with parts that we previously had, such as an ESP32 devkit, and DHT22 temperature/humidity sensors.

I am upgrading the design to keep power efficiency in mind and make the design more professional. Since it was originally programmed with the Arduino IDE, there was lots of abstraction (which was good for teaching my tech group), but now using the ESP-IDF framework and a custom PCB board, I have more control over power consumption and efficiency.

This project let me practice designing PCBs with KiCad, finding correct parts on DigiKey, and reading datasheets for the design.
